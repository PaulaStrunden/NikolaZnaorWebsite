SOMBRA je kinetički paviljon za zasjenjenje koji je projektirao MVRDV, predstavljen na Venecijanskom bijenalu arhitekture. Zasjenjenje reagira na sunčevo svjetlo bez elektronike i motora, a pokreće ga tehnologija Airshade.

Paviljon je proglašen paviljonom godine (Pavilion of the Year) na Archello Awards 2025.

Partneri: Arup, MVRDV, Metadecor, Van Rossum Raadgevend Ingenieurs, Kersten, Alumet.

Mediji: [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1029913/sombra-pavilion-mvrdv), [DETAIL](https://www.detail.de/de_en/sombra-pavillon-in-venedig-von-mvrdv), [Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/mvrdvs-sombra-pavilion-venice/), [Metalocus](https://www.metalocus.es/en/news/ode-sun-sombra-pavilion-mvrdv), [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/05/20/venice-architecture-biennale-exhibitions-off-the-beaten-track/).
