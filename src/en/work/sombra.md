SOMBRA is a kinetic shading pavilion designed by MVRDV and shown at the Venice Architecture Biennale. Its shading responds to sunlight without any electronics or motors, driven by Airshade technology.

The pavilion was named Pavilion of the Year at the Archello Awards 2025.

Partners: Arup, MVRDV, Metadecor, Van Rossum Raadgevend Ingenieurs, Kersten, Alumet.
