Competition entry for a public square and waterfront in Dubrovnik, developed with the young sculptor Teo Herceg and the stone producer Pazin Kamen. The proposal treats the square as a continuous stone surface with green garden areas. The paving pattern guides movement, marks the edges and gathers into an integrated sculptural element.

The detailing of the local stone was developed with the manufacturer so that the design could be built at full scale, referencing Dubrovnik's building heritage and its historical graphic symbols.

Sculpture: Teo Herceg · Stone: Pazin Kamen
