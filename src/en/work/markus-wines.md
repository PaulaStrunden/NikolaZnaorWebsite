The Markus Wines winery near Split is the first permanent building installation of Airshade. About 100 passive shading units protect the winery from the intense Croatian summer sun while keeping the views of the surrounding vineyard landscape.

The units move with the heat of the sun alone: sunlight warms the air inside each unit, the expanding air drives a soft actuator, and the shading turns. No sensors, no motors, no electricity.

The sketches show how the panels are hinged and moved; the photographs show the full-size prototype before installation.

Together with Aalto Architectural Bureau, the studio designed the upper floors of the building and was solely responsible for the kinetic facade. It was developed with Airshade Technologies and Alite down to each panel, joint and fixing, tested on 1:1 mock-ups, produced by Metadecor and Alumet, and supervised on site through to completion.
