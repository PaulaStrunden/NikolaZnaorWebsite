Airshade is a patented passive drive for facade shading, invented by Nikola Znaor. It is powered by the heat of the sun and the expansion of air, without sensors, cables or motors.

Sunlight heats the air chambers in the shading units. The expanding air inflates a soft robotics actuator, and this mechanical motion moves the shading. It works for new buildings as well as retrofits.

Airshade is developed by Airshade Technologies, founded in 2022 in Amsterdam, with industry partners Arup and Metadecor and academic partners AMOLF and TU Eindhoven.
